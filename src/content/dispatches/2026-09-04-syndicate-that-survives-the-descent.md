Nobody needs help syndicating a deal while it is climbing. The interesting design work
happens in the part people skip: what happens when the timeline slips, the raise comes
in light, or the operator needs a decision at 6am on a Sunday.

So I write the descent into the structure up front.

- **Milestone gates instead of vibes.** Capital moves on evidence, not enthusiasm, and everyone knows the evidence in advance.
- **One decision channel.** Limited partners get one path to a decision. Not six group chats and a rumour mill.
- **Say the uncomfortable number early.** A cap that is smaller than the original plan is survivable. A cap that nobody will name out loud is not.
- **Alignment at exit, not entry.** What each party wants on the way out is agreed before anyone signs on the way in.

Being the person who brings this up feels like a mood-killer in the room. It is also the
reason the same people come back for the next expedition.
