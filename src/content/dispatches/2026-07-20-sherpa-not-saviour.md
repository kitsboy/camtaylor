The tagline on this site says *we get people to the top — and back down again*. The back
down part is the one I care about most now.

A guide who keeps hold of the rope is not guiding. Summit fever is contagious in both
directions: founders want to keep the person who fixed things around, and consultants
are flattered enough to stay. That arrangement feels great for a quarter and then it is
a dependency dressed up as a relationship.

So I try to build the exit into the engagement:

- Every deliverable has an owner inside the company before I hand it over.
- The cap table and the decision log are legible to someone who was not in the room.
- I say the words *you do not need me for this part* out loud, on the record, when they are true.

The reward is real: the companies that no longer call me every week are the ones I
would work with again tomorrow.
