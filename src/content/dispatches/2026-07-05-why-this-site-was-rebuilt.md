The old version of this domain sat untouched for two years. That is a strange thing for
someone whose job is moving capital and companies to have as a front door, and I knew it
every time I sent the link.

So this one is built differently, on three rules:

1. **Everything on it can be checked.** The proof section reports what it can reach from your browser, the live signal section reads public chain and market data, and anything unavailable says so instead of showing a placeholder that looks like a number.
2. **The reading positions are honest.** No invented metrics, no "+340% growth" without a source, no logos I have not earned.
3. **It is fast and it is mine.** Static, self-hosted fonts, no trackers, no third-party tag manager watching a conversation between two people.

This log is the part that keeps it honest. A site with no dated entries is a brochure.
A site with them is a record.

If you are reading this later and the newest entry is old, that is on me — and it is a
useful signal in itself.
