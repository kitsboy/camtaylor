Every PropTech pitch I see is a better dashboard. OpenStrata has taught me that the
dashboard is the easy part, and it is not what makes strata management painful.

What makes it painful is that a strata corporation is a small democracy with a legal
duty, a volunteer council, a management company, and a filing cabinet of obligations
that were written by someone else, years ago, for a different building. Nobody can see
the whole thing at once, so nobody can make a clean decision.

That is an alignment problem, not a UI problem. The work is: get the council, the
manager, and the owners looking at the same object, then automate the parts that are
merely tedious (filings, minutes, notices, thresholds) so the humans can spend their
attention on the parts that are genuinely contested.

This is also why I syndicate instead of build alone. Property is a local, relationship,
legal-scale business. Software is the multiplier, not the product.
